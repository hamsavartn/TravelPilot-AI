# Constraints and Requirements

## Absolute Rules
1. **No Pre-built Solutions:** Fully pre-built solutions or previously built projects are strictly NOT allowed. All development must happen within the hackathon timeframe (19th - 20th Sept 2026).
2. **Starter Templates Allowed:** Using starter templates is acceptable.
3. **Plagiarism:** Any form of copying or plagiarism will result in disqualification.
4. **Tools & Tech Stack:** You are free to use any AI tools, APIs, and frameworks for prototyping.
5. **Team Size:** Maximum of 2 members. Solo participation is allowed.
6. **Problem Statement adherence:** All participants must build their project according to one of the provided Problem Statements. Unrelated projects will not be accepted.

## Submission Requirements
All entries must be submitted before the deadline (**20th Sept, 11:59 PM**). The submission must include:
1. **Demo Video (Mandatory):** A 3-minute demo video explaining your solution and prototype. Must be uploaded to Google Drive with link access set to public ("accessible to everyone").
2. **Codebase Access (Mandatory):** Link to the codebase (GitHub or equivalent).
3. **Solution Explanation (Mandatory):** A brief explanation of your solution.
4. **Functional Prototype:** Submissions must be fully working products, not just designs, mockups, or presentations.
5. **Contribution Clarity:** Teams must clearly specify their actual contribution vs. AI-generated output.
6. **LinkedIn Post Links:** Links to the mandatory LinkedIn posts and screenshots showing the number of likes + comments as of the submission deadline.
7. **Optional Materials:** Other materials, PPT, Live Agents Link, or N8n JSON file.

## Technical Requirements (Derived)
- **Agentic Nature:** The product must be an AI agent capable of thinking, making decisions, interacting with tools, and executing tasks independently.
- **Local Environment:** Development should happen using local directories and isolated environments (e.g., Python virtual environments) to ensure no global system clutter, as requested by the user.
